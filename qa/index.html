Questions & answers

What is Local Hub?

Local Hub is a collaborative network that partners with local businesses to educate people about common issues like water damage and leaks.

How can I access the e-book?

Here’s the link to our latest e-book: https://mylocal-hub.com/start/ebook/water-damage-guide.pdf Take a look and let us know whether you’d recommend it.

What review are you asking for?

We work with local partners to share educational materials about common issues like water damage and leaks. We’re looking for honest reviews to help us evaluate and improve those materials.

After leaving a review, what should I do?

Send us a screenshot of your feedback so we can track it on our end. If friends or family would also like to evaluate the material, you can share their contact details with their permission and we’ll get in touch. They receive a bonus for evaluating the material, and you receive a referral bonus.

Check the earlier conversation before requesting another action. Only ask for a business review about a genuine personal experience.

How should I answer a request for an update?

Read what was promised. Confirm the current status with the team, then answer that specific question with the confirmed result.

Prioritize unanswered questions and open promises. If another person needs to check something, agree who will follow up and when. Keep the conversation open until the question is resolved.

Only confirm that an action is complete after it has been verified.

What if someone asks us to stop texting?

Respect the SMS opt-out. Keep it in place and do not send further texts or add the person to another SMS follow-up.
