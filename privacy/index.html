Privacy Policy

Updated October 29, 2026

About this policy

Local Hub is a review program operated by Emergency Response Group Inc (ERG Restoration) ("we", "us"). This policy explains how we handle personal information when you sign up for Local Hub through our Facebook form, visit mylocal-hub.com, or text with us. It covers Local Hub only.

Information we collect

From the Local Hub sign-up form: your email address, full name, phone number and city.

When you take part: your text messages with us, screenshots of the reviews you post, the email address you give us for payment, and the contact details of friends you refer.

On mylocal-hub.com: what you submit on the page, and the cookie and device data described under Cookies and tracking.

For text messaging, we keep your phone number, the date you signed up, your message history and any opt-out request.

How we use information

We use this information to text you about Local Hub review tasks, send you the review link and suggested review text, check the screenshot you send, pay you and any referral bonus, answer your questions, prevent misuse, and meet legal obligations.

Sharing

We do not sell your personal information. We share it only with staff and service providers that run Local Hub for us (forms, website hosting, messaging and payments), and when the law requires it. Providers may use it only to provide their services.

Mobile information, including SMS opt-in data and consent, will not be sold or shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes. Messaging and support providers may process it solely to operate Local Hub on our behalf.

Text messages

Submitting the Local Hub form with your phone number signs you up for Local Hub texts; the form confirms that we will reach you by SMS. Message frequency varies. Message and data rates may apply. Reply STOP to unsubscribe or HELP for help. See our SMS Terms for program details.

Cookies and tracking

mylocal-hub.com uses the Meta Pixel to measure visits and sign-ups that come from our Facebook and Instagram ads. Meta may use cookies and similar technology under its own policy. You can limit ad cookies in your browser settings and in your Meta ad preferences.

Meta and other providers

When you use Facebook or Instagram, Meta also handles information under its own privacy policy, which this policy does not replace. Service providers may process information outside Canada, including in the United States, where it may be accessible under local law. We remain responsible for information handled on our behalf and use reasonable safeguards.

Retention and security

We keep personal information only as long as needed for the purposes above, legal requirements and resolving disputes. We keep opt-out records as long as needed to honor them. Information no longer needed is deleted or de-identified. We use reasonable administrative, technical and physical safeguards; no storage or transmission method is completely secure.

Access, correction and withdrawal

Contact us to ask about our privacy practices, access or correct your personal information, request deletion where applicable, or withdraw consent. We may need to verify your identity. Reply STOP to stop texts; you do not need a separate privacy request. You can also contact the Office of the Privacy Commissioner of Canada or your provincial privacy regulator.

Changes

We will post changes on this page and update the date. A change to this policy does not by itself expand the messages you agreed to receive.
