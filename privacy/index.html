Privacy Policy

Updated October 31, 2026

About this policy

Local Hub ("we", "us") operates mylocal-hub.com and the Local Hub free ebook and review pages. This policy explains how we handle personal information when you use them, including when you arrive from a Facebook or Instagram ad. It covers Local Hub only.

Information we collect

When you request the free ebook: your name, email address and city, and your phone number if you choose to add it. We also record which Local Hub page and ebook you used.

Optional feedback

If you use the optional private feedback box, we collect your message together with the contact details above. Private feedback is not posted publicly. If you choose to leave a review on Google, you do that on Google, under Google's own terms and privacy policy; we do not receive what you post unless you send it to us.

How we use information

We use this information to email you the free ebook, send occasional Local Hub updates you can unsubscribe from, answer your questions and feedback, contact you by phone or email about Local Hub if you gave us a phone number or asked us to, keep the service secure, and meet legal obligations.

Reviews and rewards

Leaving a review is optional. We invite honest reviews only, from people who have genuinely used the business. We do not require, pay for or reward a review, and any reward we offer for signing up or downloading the ebook is not tied to whether you leave a review or to what rating you give.

Sharing

We do not sell or share your personal information with third parties for marketing purposes. We share it only with staff and service providers that help us run Local Hub (for example website hosting, forms, email and customer records), and when the law requires it. Providers may use it only to provide their services.

Cookies and tracking

mylocal-hub.com uses the Meta Pixel to measure visits and ebook sign-ups that come from our Facebook and Instagram ads. Meta may use cookies and similar technology under its own policy. You can limit ad cookies in your browser settings and in your Meta ad preferences.

Meta and other providers

When you use Facebook, Instagram or Google, they also handle information under their own privacy policies, which this policy does not replace. Service providers may process information outside Canada, including in the United States, where it may be accessible under local law. We remain responsible for information handled on our behalf and use reasonable safeguards.

Retention and security

We keep personal information only as long as needed for the purposes above, legal requirements and resolving disputes. Information no longer needed is deleted or de-identified. We use reasonable administrative, technical and physical safeguards; no storage or transmission method is completely secure.

Access, correction and withdrawal

Contact us to ask about our privacy practices, access or correct your personal information, request deletion where applicable, or withdraw consent, including to emails. You can also unsubscribe from any email using the link in it. We may need to verify your identity. You can also contact the Office of the Privacy Commissioner of Canada or your provincial privacy regulator.

Changes

We will post changes on this page and update the date.
